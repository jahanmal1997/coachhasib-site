Progress photos

Required: front, back and left side. The right side is optional.

  • Wear comfortable fitted workout clothing; revealing clothing is not required.
  • Use even lighting, a plain background and a level camera. Include your whole body and feet.
  • Stand relaxed without flexing or filters. For side views, turn that shoulder toward the camera.
  • You may cover your face. See the examples below.

Choose JPG, PNG or WebP files up to 15 MB each. Photos are made smaller automatically and sent privately to your coach with your answers.
