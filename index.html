My story

From overweight to coach

I didn't start fit. I started tired, unsure, and heavier than I wanted to be.

I remember looking at old photos of myself and not liking what I saw. Nobody handed me a perfect plan. I learned by training, eating better, getting it wrong, and showing up again the next day.

That's why I coach the way I do. I don't care where you start. Thin and trying to gain muscle, or carrying extra weight and ready to lose it, the process is the same: a plan made for you, food you can actually stick to, and someone in your corner who checks in.

Every program starts with a short questionnaire about your health, goals, activity, and diet, plus front, back, and side photos. From there I write your workouts and your nutrition plan, and we adjust as you progress.

Questions

Before you start

I've never trained before. Is that okay?

Yes. Most of my clients start as beginners. Kickstart is built for that: we learn the movements first, then add weight.

I'm thin and want to gain weight. Can you help?

Yes. Gaining muscle takes enough calories, enough protein, and steady strength training. Your meal plan will set daily targets for all three.

Do I need to follow a strict diet?

No. Your plan is built around foods you already like and can afford. We adjust it as you go.

Can I change packages later?

Yes. You can move up or down at the start of any month.

What if I have an injury, condition, or allergy?

Tell me in the questionnaire. I'll plan around it, and if something needs a doctor's clearance first, I'll ask for that before we start.

I want to lose weight, not gain it. Is this for me?

Yes. The process is the same: a training plan built for you and a meal plan with the right calories and protein. For fat loss, your calories are set a little below what your body burns so you lose fat and keep your muscle.

How long is each session?

Every session is 60 minutes, including a warm-up, the main workout, and a short cool-down.

Where do we train?

In person in Sacramento. I'll confirm the gym and time when we book your first session.

What should I bring?

Comfortable workout clothes, training shoes, a water bottle, and a small towel. Eat a light meal or snack 1 to 2 hours before we start.

How soon will I see results?

Most clients feel stronger and have more energy within 2 to 4 weeks. Visible changes in your body usually show after 8 to 12 weeks of training and eating to plan. Consistency matters more than anything else.

What if I need to miss a session?

Message me at least 12 hours before and we'll move it to another day that week.

How do I pay?

By Zelle to (916) 839-8119 or in cash. Monthly packages are paid at the start of the month, before your first session.

Can I train with you if I'm not in Sacramento?

Yes. Choose the Custom Plan: a one-time $99 for a 4-week workout plan and meal plan made for you, which you follow on your own.
